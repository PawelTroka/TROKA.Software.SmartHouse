## Why the new sizes

The six-seat sauna envelope is reduced from 4.2 × 4.1 m to 3.0 × 2.4 m. That size has a direct six-person manufacturer reference. The model uses six distinct seated places and custom corner glazing; it is not a claim that RoyalSauna supplies the exact chamfered drawing at its starting price. A changing room is not included.

The seven-seat spa is slightly **larger** than the former 2.2 m square, at 2.3 m square. Seven seats must be established by the internal seating layout, not by external area alone. Recessing the spa does not remove its required service access. The default retains a factory-style sealed shell, with removable terrace panels and garden-side access. The optional overflow/infinity mode is a separate custom installation concept; the catalog reference does not authorize modification of a factory spa.

### Specimen-tree services

Purchased plant prices still use the documented stock dimensions in the existing tree catalog. Three tall Austrian pines therefore start at **3 × 59,900 = 179,700 PLN for plants**. An edited specimen outside its documented stock envelope still requires a separate plant price even when planning allowances are enabled.

With planning allowances on and no override entered:

- Planting: trees above 5 m **2,500–6,000 PLN each**; smaller trees **300–800 PLN each**.
- Anchoring: above 5 m **350–800 PLN each**; smaller **149–300 PLN each**.
- Delivery: **3,000–8,000 PLN per order** where the product reference does not include it.
- Crane / unloading: **2,500–8,000 PLN per order** where heavy handling applies.
- Establishment work: **2,000–5,000 PLN per order** with tall trees, otherwise **500–1,500 PLN**. This is temporary root-ball care / establishment work, not permanent garden irrigation or an annual maintenance model.

The ranges above are unsourced project allowances. They are explicitly separate from public plant prices. Catalog pleached-only orders do not receive duplicate delivery or automatic crane charges. Positive service fields replace these allowances; disabled trees incur no costs.

## Accounting and compatibility

`costUsePlanningAllowances` is enabled for a fresh compact or screened Sternicza design. Saved versions through v11 default to false when this setting was omitted, as do fresh Korzeniowskiego defaults. Version 12 retains its saved setting. With it off, zero means “quote required” for applicable installation lines, not free work. Historic `spa` / `spa-edge` designs do not acquire the new garden-finishes scope unless planning mode or a new garden quote is deliberately selected.

The compact and screened programs keep their garden-work scope visible when planning mode is turned off. Their missing quotes make the overall budget incomplete rather than silently deleting those costs. Positive `costSpa*` / `costGarden*` amounts replace complete lines. Product catalog amounts shown alongside them are evidence for comparison only and are not added a second time. Saved versions through v12 do not automatically acquire the new front hedge.

The combined total adds the unadjusted hedge, earthwork, specimen-tree, spa and garden ranges. It then applies the selected uncertainty percentage **once, to the upper bound only**. All detail tables show their amounts before that percentage. Hedges and soil retain their existing quantities, independent side controls, soil-volume partitioning and editable rates.
