## OLED and Dolby Vision: reservation versus selected product

The model reserves a nominal **85-inch, 16:9 screen rectangle**, approximately **1.882 × 1.058 m**, centred **1.28 m** above the floor. It does not claim that a specific 85-inch OLED with the requested Dolby Vision generation has been selected.

The official **Philips 83OLED811/12** sheet identifies an **83-inch OLED** and **Dolby Vision 2 Max**, making it a relevant candidate for the requested feature set. However, that same sheet also prints **194 cm** and **1,714 × 978 mm**, which are inconsistent with an 83-inch product. Consequently, this model does not use those product dimensions as a confirmed installation envelope. Confirm the released model and final installation sheet before specifying the display. [Philips official sheet, dated 9 July 2026](https://www.documents.philips.com/assets/20260709/bcb44f0f8a8d49e2a838b482009d0bba.pdf)

The room therefore retains the larger 85-inch planning reservation, while product notes identify the 83-inch candidate and the verification still required. Equipment labels use “target” and “design envelope” consistently.

## 7.2.4 Atmos geometry

A, B and D each contain **13 separate audio devices**: seven ear-level channels, two independent subwoofers and four flush ceiling speakers. Eleven speaker channels require amplification; the two powered subwoofers use their own outputs. C contains no cinema audio devices.

Placement is measured from one defined listening point at **1.05 m seated ear height**. The layout rotates with the screen; switching sides does not leave the speakers in their old positions. The center speaker sits immediately below the display and is aimed toward the listener. The remaining six ear-level speakers use the listener's ear height.

| Channels | Placement relative to the listening direction |
| --- | --- |
| Front left / right | **−25° / +25°** in plan. |
| Center | **0°** in plan, below the screen. |
| Side surrounds | **−100° / +100°** in plan. |
| Rear surrounds | **−140° / +140°** in plan. |
| Top front left / right | About **48.2°** in the longitudinal side view at the default ceiling. |
| Top rear left / right | About **131.8°** in that same side view. |
| Two subwoofers | Separated floor positions, with final placement and delay/bass calibration determined in the finished room. |

The overhead directions are within Dolby's illustrated **30–55° front** and **125–150° rear** side-view ranges. Their actual three-dimensional elevation is about **44.0°** in the default room, since they also sit to the listener's left and right. The distinction avoids confusing a side projection with a spatial angle. [Dolby 7.1.4 overhead setup guide](https://www.dolby.com/about/support/guide/speaker-setup-guides/7.1.4-overhead-speaker-setup-guide) · [Dolby Atmos home installation guidelines](https://www.dolby.com/siteassets/technologies/dolby-atmos/atmos-installation-guidelines-121318_r3.1.pdf?pdf=Installation-Guidelines-)

The ceiling speakers have **240 mm** envelopes. Their positions follow the edited ceiling height, and the decorative ceiling boards/battens leave physical apertures around them. They belong to the ceiling visibility group, so removing the ceiling in a dollhouse view also removes the overhead hardware. Front channels use discreet wall grilles; surrounds without a supporting wall use slim **220 mm** stand footprints. Each subwoofer reserves **440 × 440 × 460 mm**.

The **2.34 × 0.60 m**, **540 mm high** AV cabinet has an open center receiver bay and no rear panel across that bay. The receiver envelope is **434 W × 482 D × 195 H mm**, using the **Denon AVC-A10H** with horizontal antennas as the size reference. That receiver has 13 amplified channels; the proposed 7.2.4 arrangement uses eleven. [Denon product source](https://www.denon.com/en/product/av-receivers/avc-a10h/AVCA10HBKE2.html) The model leaves approximately **113 mm** to either side of the receiver and **175–180 mm** above it. The cabinet does not represent a sealed equipment box. Exact cooling, cable bend and service allowances follow the chosen receiver's documentation.

The integrated media panel is approximately **3.02 m wide**, with timber and fabric finishes supplied by the selected style. Generic feature-wall art does not appear over the screen or speakers.
