# Sternicza 11: a compact pine grove shifted toward the left boundary

Date: **13 November 2026**. Programme: **spa-compact**, with a **3.0 × 2.4 m sauna** and **2.3 × 2.3 m seven-seat jacuzzi**. Left means physical **east when looking from the house into the rear garden**. This comparison changes tree placement while holding the compact spa, mapped terrain, buildings and observer assumptions constant.

## Selected compact left-edge grove

The default preserves the requested close grouping at the rear-left pool end. The outer pine moves **0.50 m farther left/east**, the rear inner pine moves **0.20 m left/east and 0.05 m rearward**, and the middle pine stays in its published version 1.9 position. No default tree moves forward toward the house.

The trunk centres occupy **4.80 × 3.50 m**, compared with **4.50 × 3.50 m** previously. Pairwise distances are **3.65, 4.80 and 5.13 m**. Pine 1's nominal 5 m crown overlaps each of the other two crowns, forming a **connected group**; pines 2 and 3 do not overlap each other directly. The total nominal crown envelope is approximately **9.80 × 8.50 m**. This preserves the compact composition while moving its outer edge left.

All three trunks satisfy the study's **1 m conceptual centre-to-boundary and centre-to-ground-feature allowances**, and remain clear of occupied observer locations. These allowances do not establish the actual root-ball size, foundation clearance, lifting access or long-term root compatibility of purchased 9.5 m specimens.

**The outer crown extends approximately 0.29 m beyond the left/east parcel boundary**, while its trunk is about **2.21 m inside**. The simulator draws and credits that full crown and explicitly discloses the overhang. It is not an approved planting right. **Contained corner** retains the previous grove with all modeled crowns inside the parcel.

The strip between the east pool coping and the parcel edge is only **about 0.82 m** wide. Moving the middle trunk directly left would conflict with the pool's conceptual planting allowance. Moving it behind the pool instead lowers the crown's apparent height from the elevated living room and changes its alignment with the outdoor sightlines. Whole-group east/rear translation therefore does not provide a simple improvement from every view.

The catalog plant reference remains **59,900 PLN each / 179,700 PLN for all three**. Delivery, lifting, planting, anchoring and establishment are separate budget items. This study creates no nursery reservation or contractor quotation.

## Horizontal alignment and height are separate constraints

The leftward shift intercepts some additional oblique PCK3 rays. The rear trees remain far from the raised living room, so their crowns appear lower than a nearer screening tree.

| Maximum elevation angle | Living room seated | Living room standing |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unscreened PCK3 target sample | 26.08° | 25.46° |
| Rear inner pine 1 crown | 22.64° | 21.46° |
| Middle pine 2 crown | 26.35° | 24.99° |
| Outer pine 3 crown | 21.64° | 20.52° |

These are maximum silhouette angles of modeled ellipsoids. The highest crown point is not necessarily aligned with the highest target ray; the crown's sides are lower. One crown exceeding the target's maximum elevation angle does not imply full screening.

Ignoring tree intersections whose first entry lies outside the parcel leaves **all 17 selected-default counts unchanged**, including **202/420 seated**, **216/420 standing**, **46/460 jacuzzi** and **1/420 garden** visible samples. This diagnoses dependence on outside crown volume; it does not specify how a real crown could be pruned without altering screening.

## Reproduction and limits

Run:

```sh
node scripts/search-left-edge-pck3-layouts.mjs
```

The selected default is a deliberate mild adjustment of the published grove, prioritizing **compact rear grouping and connected crowns**. It is not the arrangement with the highest visibility score. The script reproduces it directly alongside the wider comparison and all historical comparisons.

The separate wider-placement search uses **x 13.5–19.3 m / y 29.0–37.1 m**, a 0.25 m grid, a 64-state beam, three coordinate passes and 0.1 m refinement. Its tree spacings are 3–7 m, and each candidate reserves the stated 1 m conceptual allowances and avoids occupied viewpoints. The scoring combines living-room, jacuzzi and garden coverage with the other occupied areas. The selected compact grove's first trunk at x=13.3 m is intentionally outside that wider-search region; it is independently checked against the same actual planting allowances.

The study records **17 viewpoints for eight arrangements** and **25 sunlight snapshots**. Every reported ray count is independently checked with `analyzeVisibility`. Complete settings and results are in [left-edge-screening-comparison.json](left-edge-screening-comparison.json).

The proposal uses **4 m hedges**, **0.5 m uniform garden fill over mapped slope**, **1.2 m assumed living-floor height**, **4.2 m terrace depth**, no canopy, removal of the detached garage, and exclusion of unverified high rooftop returns. Buildings and stock crowns are not resized to improve the result.

As a diagnostic, unioning all **336** accepted grid, refined and comparison crowns while ignoring spacing and tree count still leaves **146/420 seated living-room** and **162/420 standing** samples visible. This describes a sampled set only; it is not a feasible design or a mathematical proof about all continuous placements.

The model treats crown volumes as opaque. Actual foliage gaps, stem form, establishment and pruning can change privacy and sunlight. Existing purchased crown sizes are used; the results do not depend on several years of tree growth.
