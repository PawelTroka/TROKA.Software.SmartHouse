# Garden design styles for the three Gdańsk properties

## What each concept represents

Each concept is an original garden proposal. The catalogue translates a small set of architectural precedents into repeatable choices of paving module, seating construction, canopy trim and luminaire form. In the model a metre of timber board, a metre of clinker paving and a metre of large-format slab have different joint rhythms. Style selection uses the active property’s terrace, tub cutout, furniture positions and light schedule.

The optional house exterior preview is a separate colour study on the owner's building. Existing roof pitches, window openings, house orientation, neighbouring facades and survey records remain in their own geometry. Procurement, labour, finishes and bespoke fabrication require product-specific quotations. The budget keeps the base finish allowances and provides a separate incremental style quote. Its default of zero means the upgrade is unquoted.

## Design precedents and their interpretation

| Organic Modern | Fallingwater’s conservation team describes an interplay of layered local stone, smooth concrete terraces and curved details. [Western Pennsylvania Conservancy](https://fallingwater.org/learn/preservation-and-collections/preservation-history/) | Butt-jointed courses vary in length. Rounded upholstery sits on a stone-like plinth, beneath horizontal fascia bands. |

[Fundació Mies van der Rohe](https://miesbcn.com/the-pavilion/) | Aligned large panels, thin sled frames and a single fascia reveal. Exterior slip-tested paving would be needed to achieve the visual effect beside water. |
| Luxury Hidden Bali Resort | Amankila presents individual pavilions opening onto private terraces. Bali’s subak is a living community water-management landscape with spiritual significance. [Amankila](https://www.aman.com/resorts/amankila) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1194/) | The resort’s sheltered outdoor rooms inform the close soffit battens, daybed with a woven apron and warm basket fittings. The proposal adds no planted palm, sacred motif, water terrace or increased privacy credit. |
| Mediterranean Villa | Villa Kérylos arranges its social rooms around a peristyle open to the sea. Villa d’Este uses built terraces, ornamental basins and architecture to organise an outdoor sequence. [Villa Kérylos](https://www.villakerylos.fr/en/home/) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1025/) | The existing covered terrace becomes the focus through terra-coloured basket paving, olive metal loops and a stepped edge. | Scandinavian and Danish Mid-century | Designmuseum Danmark traces Danish furniture traditions from functional interiors to later experimental forms. The Aalto Foundation records lightweight furniture, controlled timber bending and the transition to warm natural finishes. [Designmuseum Danmark](https://designmuseum.dk/en/exhibition/danish-modern/) · [Aalto House](https://www.alvaraalto.fi/en/architecture/the-aalto-house/) | Scandinavian uses pale rectangular slats and regular ribs. The Danish variant contrasts tapered legs, amber fabric and open saucers with a staggered paved floor. |
| Organic Scandinavian | Aalto’s cantilever armchair employed thin timber pieces glued and bent in a mould. [Alvar Aalto Foundation](https://www.alvaraalto.fi/en/work/armchair-42/) | Angled timber runner frames, pillowed profiles and cross-laid pale boards give this version a softer profile. Any outdoor laminated component needs suitable construction and adhesive. |
| Japandi and Organic Japandi | The Met’s shōin room records the proportions and modular floor treatment of a Japanese interior. Portland Japanese Garden describes the aesthetic use of blank space in its dry landscape garden. [The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/640986) · [Portland Japanese Garden](https://japanesegarden.org/garden-spaces/sand-stone-garden/) | These contemporary hybrids use the existing open area. Japandi has alternating two-to-one modules, a rectangular platform and square lantern. Organic Japandi has unequal courses, softer low seats and an open ribbed oval lantern. |
| Space Age | Aalto University describes Futuro’s elliptical form and the late-1960s experiments with plastics and fabrication. [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/futuro-houses-presented-at-espoos-weegee) | Rounded shells sit on drum pedestals, with opal globe forms and diamond paving. The moulding and metallic trim are unpriced fabrication ideas. |
| Luxury Contemporary Party Club | Vitra Design Museum documents modular club interiors and the close relationship between furniture, lighting and performance. [Vitra Design Museum](https://www.design-museum.de/en/exhibitions/detailpages/night-fever-designing-club-culture-1960-today.html) | Channelled upholstery, a banded floor and coloured inlays create a social setting. The shade is tiered and the existing switches still govern its warm lens. |
| Baltic Dune House and Gdańsk Brick Courtyard | The coastal national park describes the movement of sand and formation of parallel dune ridges. Gdańsk’s National Maritime Museum records the waterfront crane’s role as both building and equipment. [Słowiński National Park](https://spn.gov.pl/pliki-do-pobrania/otworz/5cfc9a3b-cf6a-4e9b-96b4-0c24eee83ff1.pdf) · [National Maritime Museum](https://nmm.pl/en/crane/) | Silvered cross-boards and canvas refer to the Baltic coast. Small red clinker courses, trestle seating and dark riveted trim form the urban courtyard. Neither proposal requires importing beach material. |
| Finnish Sauna Terrace | At Muuratsalo the Aaltos combined a smoke sauna, open court and experiments in brick and ceramic finishes. [Alvar Aalto Foundation](https://www.alvaraalto.fi/en/architecture/muuratsalo-experimental-house/) | The terrace furniture borrows the narrow slat rhythm of a sauna bench. Programme selection continues to determine whether the site includes a sauna. |
| Riviera Art Deco | Miami Beach’s conservation material documents the use of geometric lines, pastel palettes and portholes in coastal Art Deco. [City of Miami Beach](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/this-month-in-miami-beach-history-the-business-of-preservation-begins/) | An original resort interpretation uses stepped bases, a triple cornice and layered shades. |
| Arts and Crafts Garden | The V&A documents the movement’s interest in material knowledge and construction. Historic England describes an Arts and Crafts garden composed of smaller outdoor rooms. [V&A](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/arts-and-crafts-an-introduction) · [Historic England](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1467139) | Timber trestles, peg details and basketweave brick are the main visible changes. | Palm Springs Poolside and Desert Modern | Palm Springs Art Museum describes the steel frame, concrete podium and sun-control overhangs of Albert Frey’s second house and its response to a measured site. [Palm Springs Art Museum](https://www.psmuseum.org/visit/frey-house1) | Poolside uses bright slings and a coral profile. [Ballenberg](https://ballenberg.ch/en/discover-the-museum/architecture-and-buildings/) · [Stiftung Bauhaus Dessau](https://bauhaus-dessau.de/en/venues/bauhaus-building/) | Broad boards and crossed timber braces distinguish the lodge. ## Gdańsk exposure and procurement

- **Winter exposure.** IMGW’s 1991–2020 normal for the Gdańsk station gives a mean daily minimum of −3.3 °C in January and −2.7 °C in February. These are station statistics. A pool surround or sheltered courtyard may have a different surface temperature and freeze pattern. [IMGW minimum temperature](https://klimat.imgw.pl/pl/climate-normals/TMIN_AVE)
- **Walking and splash surfaces.** Specify the exact exterior product, declared frost behaviour, and suitable wet or barefoot slip performance for the location. Include joints, falls, drainage and edge detailing in the installed quote. As an example of the available evidence, Paradyż publishes frost resistance, the R10 slip class and outdoor joint instructions for a named clinker product. That data describes the listed variant. Poolside barefoot use needs its own product assessment. [Ceramika Paradyż](https://www.paradyz.com/en/products/p65716-sundown-terra-mat-elewacja-245x66-g1)
- **Boards and soffits.** Allow for drainage below timber decking, ventilation, swelling gaps, appropriate fixings and access for cleaning. Board spacing and joint width in the visual are illustrative. Use the chosen system’s installation guide for the eventual assembly. [Thermory installation guide](https://thermory.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Final-with-onepager_25.03.24_Thermory_INSTALLATION_GUIDE_decking-boards_0324_ENG.pdf)
- **Exposed metal.** Salt deposition, sheltered surfaces and the frequency of rain washing influence grade and maintenance requirements. A coating or metallic colour in the viewer gives no corrosion-resistance rating. Ask for the exposure and maintenance assumptions for each proposed finish, especially sheltered fascia, fasteners and areas beside chlorinated water. [Outokumpu](https://www.outokumpu.com/en/expertise/2025/stainless-steel-and-atmospheric-corrosion-what-you-need-to-know)
- **Plants.** Coastal wind and salt exposure vary with shelter, and plant hardiness depends on local winter conditions. The Bali, Mediterranean and desert-inspired concepts derive their character from materials, light and shade geometry. The selected Polish garden programme and planting selections carry through unchanged. [RHS coastal planting](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/for-places/coastal-areas) · [IMGW climate normals](https://klimat.imgw.pl/pl/climate-normals/)
- **Outdoor lighting.** DarkSky and the Illuminating Engineering Society recommend targeted, low-level, controlled and warm-coloured lighting. The proposals carry the existing circuit locations, intensities and independent dimmers. A warm lens or shield shape illustrates an intention. A selected fixture and measured aiming, spill and illuminance would be required to assess installed performance. [DarkSky / IES](https://darksky.org/resources/guides-and-how-tos/lighting-principles/)

## Comparing concepts in the simulator

Hold the property, programme, view, chosen light circuits, date and time constant. From an elevated garden view compare the paving module against the tub opening and the outline of each daybed. From eye level compare joinery, underside battens, fascia and pendant profile. Switch the exterior preview on separately to test coordinated house colours. A change in dimensions, observers, screening or real fixtures should be made through its own control.

All procedural meshes and colours in these concepts were authored for Garden Lab. Precedent images, vendor textures and historic furniture models are not bundled.
