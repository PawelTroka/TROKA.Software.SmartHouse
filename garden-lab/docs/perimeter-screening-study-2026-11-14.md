# PCK3 screening without trees in the middle of the garden

**Sternicza 11 · 14 November 2026 · Garden Lab 1.15.1**

## What the work establishes

The three trunk centres in the supplied picture are the reference: T1 **(19.30, 30.20)**, T2 **(13.80, 35.80)** and T3 **(18.45, 35.57)** metres in the local mapped frame. All three are **10 m high**; crown diameters are 6, 6 and 5 m. The new explicit application action makes these coordinates reviewable and preserves them across reloads, including when the opened URL contained an earlier design. The current pool remains the previously requested **3.5 × 8.0 m** programme. The supplied older picture contains the 6 m pool; this explains a substantial difference in the visual relationship between the pool and otherwise matching tree centres.

The research does **not** establish a ready-to-order three-tree arrangement that hides PCK3 from every position within one year. It identifies promising specialist stock and a stronger perimeter-focused combination, while exposing the space and foliage requirements that a construction design must satisfy.

The strongest of the tested combinations is the three pictured centres with **11 m height and 7 m crown diameters**, one additional **10 × 6 m pine at (11.00, 35.20)** in the rear planting strip, and a finite terrace canopy with **0.6 m fascia**. This intercepts every sampled PCK3 façade and roof ray from eight of nine fixed eyes. The standing living-room eye still has **129 of 5,214 façade samples** and **28 of 365 roof samples** exposed. Actual plants have gaps, irregularity and variable density, so this remains an optimistic geometric concept. The source reference design is not replaced with this alternative.

## What the comparison means

Simply lifting the current 6/6/5 m crowns from 10 to 11 or 12 m does not address the dining area's horizontal gap. Increasing actual crown width helps the house and jacuzzi views, but a separate rear-edge crown is much more effective for that dining gap. This is why the rear extra is placed at (11.00, 35.20), rather than in the middle of the lawn.

The extra-tree search evaluated 288 positions in two limited strips: east of the terrace before the pool, and beside the rear boundary. It retained 108 positions after checking **26 finished, access and furniture envelopes**, a 1.5 m trunk-centre margin to those envelopes and the boundary, and 2.8 m between trunk centres. The selected rear extra is 2.027 m from the cold-plunge pad, its nearest finished envelope, and 2.836 m from the boundary. A second extra at (17.00, 24.40) helps the living-room views only modestly.

These margins establish optical comparison candidates; they do not certify planting holes, mature roots, foundations, maintenance access or an arboricultural planting design. In particular, a 2.7 m root-ball disc with 1.5 m centre clearance has only 0.15 m nominal clearance beyond its radius.

### The strongest nursery lead for a taller, narrower screen

[Von Falkenhayn's giant Thuja plicata ‘Excelsa’ catalogue](https://www.von-falkenhayn.de/sortiment/detail/solitaerbaeume-von-thuja-plicata-excelsa-grosser-lebensbaum-2) lists **12–13 m height and 3–3.5 m maximum crown width**, with about **2.7 m root-ball diameter and 12 t mass**. No project price, tagged plants, quantity or delivery date was obtained. No 13–14 m stock was verified.

The [Polish Nurserymen Association](https://e-katalogroslin.pl/plants/7201,zywotnik-olbrzymi-excelsa_thuja-plicata-excelsa) describes loose branching and sparse basal branching, with increasing density as the plant ages. The top is tapered. Maximum crown width therefore cannot be interpreted as that width at 12–13 m height, nor as guaranteed opaque foliage.

A deliberately schematic replacement row of five **13 × 3.5 m opaque cones** was tested in the physically admissible strips. It is **not** a species-accurate Excelsa model. Because the pool prevents a continuous row along the important east-side sector and the cones are narrow near their tops, it hides only about 54–56% of façade samples from the principal house/terrace/jacuzzi eyes. This is a warning against replacing broad crowns on height alone, not proof that every possible Excelsa design will fail. The five cones have at least 2.85 m centre spacing; two rear crowns nominally overhang the boundary/coping by less than 0.09 m.

## The pool and giant-tree space conflict

The shortest distance from the **full pool coping to the east boundary is 2.0029 m**. The reference T1 and T3 also have approximately 1 m trunk-centre-to-coping gaps. These are conceptual visual dimensions.

[Von Falkenhayn's documented pine examples](https://www.von-falkenhayn.de/sortiment/detail/solitaerbaeume-von-pinus-nigra-ssp-nigra-oesterreichische-schwarzkiefer) have about 2.6 m balls at 9–10 m tree height and 2.7 m balls at 10–11 m. These are that nursery's examples, not confirmed root-ball dimensions for Galeria Ogrodowa's products. The giant Excelsa class likewise lists a 2.7 m ball. Such a ball cannot fit wholly inside a 2.00 m strip, before allowing any digging room. T1's approximately 1 m boundary distance also needs review for those specimen classes.

For a continuous giant-tree edge screen, the pool and planting strip would need a coordinated redesign. Moving the pool inward can create space without moving trees into the central lawn, but exact dimensions must follow the selected trees, their root balls and the excavation/support method. A nursery's maximum crown size does not establish a safe universal pool-to-tree distance.

[Van den Berk's planting guidance](https://www.vdberk.com/index.php/advice/planting-trees/) recommends a planting-hole diameter around 1.5–2 times the root-ball diameter and allowance for heavy balls settling. For a 2.7 m ball this means a general preparation diameter of about 4.05–5.4 m, not permission to undermine a neighbouring foundation or pool shell. Establish the proposed raised grade first and plant at the correct root-flare depth; do not bury an existing trunk under the garden fill. The chosen installation team must resolve the actual root zone, lifting access, drainage, anchoring and aftercare.

## How tall a boundary screen must be

The critical height is measured **above the local proposed planting soil**, after the single 0.5 m garden raise. For each tested ray:

`required screen height = ray elevation at the hedge-line crossing − (original local terrain elevation + applicable garden/bed fill)`.

The required top is approximately **13.15 m** for the most demanding tested standing living-room ray. A continuous ideal opaque 13.2 m surface along the left/east and rear hedge lines intercepts all tested façade and roof samples. This has only about **5 cm numerical headroom**, so it is not a practical procurement target with adequate survey, wind, foliage or construction tolerance. A 13 m tree leader is not the same thing as an opaque 13 m surface extending across the required horizontal sector.

The boundary calculations temporarily remove the three pines, so they show the complete required edge envelope rather than giving a crown arbitrary credit. All relevant uncovered rays cross the left/east or rear lines in the model. Source terrain, building heights and the assumed house-floor level still require physical confirmation before purchasing specimen trees.

## A terrace screen can address the difficult occupied views

A closed roof with the correct fascia and side returns can intercept high sightlines close to an observer. This is a useful complement to perimeter planting. The comparison's canopy is a finite model surface, not a selected proprietary product or an engineered roof. The standing living-room residual demonstrates why a roof alone should not be promised to hide the entire building; side-screen location and angle need their own sightline check.

[Renson's official integrated-screen information](https://renson.net/en-gb/products/pergolas/side-elements/integrated-fixscreen) offers fabrics with different transparency. A solar screen must therefore not be treated as automatically opaque. Specify the required visual effect with a physical material sample; closed solid infill or correctly oriented louvres are a different optical assumption from mesh. Consider the selected system's operating wind limits, especially where a retractable screen is expected to remain down.

[Viven Garden's Camargue listing](https://vivengarden.pl/produkt/pergola-camargue/) gives a published **88,590–114,350 PLN** range, including 23% VAT, for the roof on four posts **without equipment**. It does not quote the simulator's full 8.5 × 4.2 m terrace, opaque side panels, foundations or installation as a complete scope. The actual proposal needs a dimensioned supplier offer. Its cost should not be silently substituted into the current garden budget.

## The blank 4h wall needs a separate design response

The model retains the user's observation that the garden-facing wall has no windows. Its approximately 10.13 m source height makes a standard 4 m hedge insufficient to conceal the upper part. The companion study recommends an owner-agreed repair and muted repaint as the immediate improvement, followed by selected evergreen climbing panels. The entire gross blank elevation is about 134.3 m²; only about 11.4 m of its 13.26 m length runs alongside this plot, so the actual treatment area must be measured separately.

‘Białystok’ ivy is an appropriate cultivar to investigate for the longer-term layer, not an instant 10 m cover: its Polish breeder lists evergreen foliage, shade/partial-shade suitability and approximately 1 m annual growth. Independent frames provide an option when attachment to the wall is unavailable, but a wall-height frame needs structural and planning review. A modular living wall provides a much more immediate green surface at substantially greater cost and service demand. Full quotations and owner approval have not been obtained.

## Decision recommended now

Use the exact pictured three-tree layout as the common visual reference. Develop two fully costed alternatives from measured stock: (1) broader/taller corner pines plus a rear-edge tree and targeted terrace screening, and (2) a genuinely continuous tall edge screen with a redesigned pool/planting strip. The first currently performs better in the available-footprint comparison; the second is the appropriate investigation if complete perimeter-only concealment is essential.

Before committing, survey the house floor, eye positions and relevant roof corners; obtain tagged photographs and crown widths at several heights; confirm root balls and delivery access; then rerun the geometry using conservative live-foliage shapes. The procurement objective is a verified view from the actual sofa, standing living room, jacuzzi and dining seats, rather than a purchase based only on total tree height.

No suppliers were contacted, no stock was reserved and no site-specific quotations were received in this research. Published prices are anchors with their stated scope and tax basis. The default budget and tree count are not changed to imply that the research alternatives are priced or approved.
