## Horizontal fit

Checks use the outer coping, individual stair footprints, finite columns and panels, the chamfered sauna, the U-shaped lounge edge, the cold-plunge pad, the tub shell and its separate service zone. A canopy roof and the projected balcony members also count towards parcel containment. The optional basement checks the retaining walls at the frontage, the proposed basement room against the mapped building footprint, and the existing full-drive assessment. Joined terrace thresholds, tub openings and deck-supported columns have explicit interfaces. A plan overlap identifies a conflict between the checked component footprints. Loads, buried services, root systems and foundations need additional evidence.

The polygon intersection divides the shapes at every vertex and edge-crossing height. Within each resulting strip, the paired interior spans vary linearly, so midpoint integration gives the overlap area for simple, concave or convex rings within numerical tolerance. A shared edge or corner has zero overlap area. This catches a long beam or coping cutting across a boundary notch even when its corners stay inside. A wholly exterior feature with a positive edge-to-edge distance is labelled outside in the dimension schedule.

## Water, shadows and the observer

All pools use one horizontal water plane. Ground is sampled over the proposed 0.28 m coping bands, up to 0.25 m apart, using the bilinearly interpolated native terrain and the selected fill. The coping top is 75 mm above the highest grade sample and the skirt base 80 mm below the lowest. Fresh designs assume a skimmer operating waterline 100 mm below the coping. The selectable freeboard spans 20–250 mm, and pre-v24 scenarios reopen at their former 20 mm freeboard. The chosen level moves the water, basin floor, swimmer and light calculations together. It is independent of the jacuzzi overflow setting. The 100 mm value is a design assumption. Ospa's HL indicates 100 mm including finished flooring, AstralPool's purpose-designed SLIM high-water system approximately 40 mm, and RivieraPool's MLine example approximately 60 mm below the coping. A 20 mm near-flush design needs its own specified high-water or overflow system. [Ospa surface cleaner](https://www.ospa-schwimmbadtechnik.de/en/water-treatment/surface-cleaner-skimmer) · [AstralPool SLIM](https://www.astralpool.com/en/en/pool-shell-equipment/skimmers/skimmer-15l/slim-high-water-level-skimmer.html) · [RivieraPool MLine](https://de.rivierapool.com/custom-made)

 The conceptual water depth is 1.40 m. Only ground beneath the coping sets this water level. A flat pool set solely at its centre previously buried roughly 95 mm of the legacy Sternicza coping on the uphill side. The same shortcut left the skirt hanging above the low ground at the other two sites. The corrected levels apply to classic and spa layouts and to shared designs when reopened. Terrain resolution, focused field levels, groundwater and drainage still govern the actual pool design.

Water, the swimmer’s selectable eye offset and sun receivers use the same water elevation datum. A low swimmer can look up over the lip while a shallow ray can meet the coping or inner basin wall. Direct-sun percentages count sunlit points on the 1 m outdoor sample grid at the selected local date and time. Where a sampled cell is assigned to water, its ray starts within the clipped water portion so a zero-width margin cannot stand in for a whole cell. Shade includes the pool coping and terrace steps in every garden programme. Every eligible sample has equal weight. Irradiance, lux, daily hours and weather would require different calculations.

Privacy percentages count blocked rays from a single eye to unweighted target façade and roof samples. The model excludes self-hidden synthetic rear-façade points from that denominator and retains measured roof returns conservatively. A finite canopy post blocks a ray intersecting its volume. Rays just beside it pass. Optional balcony glass gives no opaque credit. Own-house occlusion in the privacy calculation uses a mapped building volume, while the solar and rendered roof use the more detailed roof geometry. Sternicza's photographed cross-gable overrides conflicting roof samples locally. Unmeasured windows and open-glazing assumptions limit interpretations of either result.
