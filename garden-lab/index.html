    THE MODEL, EXPLAINED

    Measured context.
    Adjustable design.

    This is a planning simulator for Sternicza 11. The site and surrounding buildings are derived from public map and elevation data; the renovation is a proposal.

    What the model knows

    • Combined parcel outlines 401 and 400: about 20.3 m wide and 38.2 m deep. Inclusion of parcel 400 is a design assumption, not proof of ownership.
    • Main house and garage footprints, surrounding apartment footprints, a 1 m terrain grid and selected 2024 laser-scanned roof returns.
    • The front mapped house outline is about 9.1 m from the street-side parcel boundary. This is not a surveyed curb-to-wall measurement.

    What you can change

    Independent hedge heights, planting beds, garden fill, floor and eye heights, observer position, terrace depth and canopy geometry. Optional building height and distance adjustments are comparison scenarios; resetting them restores the mapped data exactly. Budget inputs and building changes are included in shared links and scenario comparisons. The house floor stays fixed when you raise the garden. New hedges move with the fill only when the corresponding switch is on.

    How screening is calculated

    Rays run from the selected eye position to sampled facade and roof points. Finite hedge screens, the terrace canopy and approximate surrounding building volumes can intercept them. The percentage is the share of those sampled points blocked; it is not the percentage of your visual field or a guarantee for every point in the garden.

    The house/window openings and ornamental planting are not reliable surveyed obstructions, so they do not contribute to the coverage score. The thin high returns near PCK 3 are excluded by default; enable them in the assumptions for a sensitivity check. Nearby roof points include sampled rooftop details; the two farther blocks use only median roof levels. The score covers all five target buildings from the observer, regardless of the camera direction.

    Orientation

    Left and right mean looking from the house into the rear garden, toward SSW. The existing garage and PCK 3 are on the left/east; the west apartment block is on the right/west. Plans keep the street at the top and show true north. Older saved hedge heights and prices are migrated to preserve their geographic side.

    Geometry & proposals

    The 8.5 m terrace, pool, jacuzzi, glazing, architectural finishes and furniture illustrate the design. House and garage roof meshes are approximations of the available mapped roof returns; they stay fixed when the assumed living-floor level changes. Canopy rays use a finite roof and front/side fascia. The budget derives rear-garden fill quantities after excluding the pool, terrace and any retained garage. Soil, placing, delivery, access, drainage and retaining allowances are editable. Quantities are planning estimates; retaining and drainage allowances are not an engineered design. Thuja pricing uses published size bands up to the verified limit, with editable supplier quotes for larger stock.

    Sun & shade

    The sun position is calculated for 54.405452° N, 18.6312142° E using NOAA/Meeus equations and Europe/Warsaw local time, including daylight saving. The plot orientation is converted from grid north to true north; the rear axis is about 203.13° true. Date and time, sun-path visibility and lighting mode are saved with each scenario.

    The shade map traces rays from outdoor points on a 1 m grid toward the geometric sun centre. It includes the modeled house and garage roofs, mapped surrounding buildings, opaque hedge screens, raised beds, terrace canopy and supplied local terrain. Pool and jacuzzi water have flat receiving levels; their excavations and terrace opening are included. Its percentages describe sample points at the selected moment, not daily sunlight hours, energy or exact area. Roof meshes interpolate available source returns; proposed structures and finishes remain illustrative. Existing tree crowns, ornamental planting, furniture, distant terrain horizons, clouds, diffuse light and reflections are not part of the numerical score. Small decorative shadows may still appear in the 3D illustration. Night is reported separately from architectural shade.
