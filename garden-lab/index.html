THE MODEL, EXPLAINED

Measured context.
Adjustable design.

This is a planning simulator for Sternicza 11. The site and surrounding buildings are derived from public map and elevation data; the renovation is a proposal.

What the model knows

  • Combined parcel outlines 401 and 400: about 20.3 m wide and 38.2 m deep. Inclusion of parcel 400 is a design assumption, not proof of ownership.
  • Main house and garage footprints, surrounding apartment footprints, a 1 m terrain grid and selected 2024 laser-scanned roof returns.
  • The front mapped house outline is about 9.1 m from the street-side parcel boundary. This is not a surveyed curb-to-wall measurement.

What you can change

Hedge heights, planting beds, garden fill, floor and eye heights, observer position, terrace depth and canopy geometry. The house floor stays fixed when you raise the garden. New hedges move with the fill only when the corresponding switch is on.

How screening is calculated

Rays run from the selected eye position to sampled facade and roof points. Finite hedge screens, the terrace canopy and approximate surrounding building volumes can intercept them. The percentage is the share of those sampled points blocked; it is not the percentage of your visual field or a guarantee for every point in the garden.

The house/window openings and ornamental planting are not reliable surveyed obstructions, so they do not contribute to the coverage score. The thin high returns near PCK 3 are excluded by default; enable them in the assumptions for a sensitivity check. Nearby roof points include sampled rooftop details; the two farther blocks use only median roof levels. The score covers all five target buildings from the observer, regardless of the camera direction.

Geometry & proposals

The 8.5 m terrace, pool, jacuzzi, glazing, architectural finishes and furniture illustrate the design. The pitched roof is schematic. Canopy rays use a finite roof and front/side fascia. Earthworks volumes are not a construction specification; drainage and retaining details are outside this model.
