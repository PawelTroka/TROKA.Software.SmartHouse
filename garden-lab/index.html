    THE MODEL, EXPLAINED

    Measured context.
    Adjustable design.

    This is a planning simulator for Sternicza 11. The site and surrounding buildings are derived from public map and elevation data; the renovation is a proposal.

    What the model knows

    • Combined parcel outlines 401 and 400: about 20.3 m wide and 38.2 m deep. Inclusion of parcel 400 is a design assumption, not proof of ownership.
    • Main house and garage footprints, surrounding apartment footprints, a 1 m terrain grid and selected 2024 laser-scanned roof returns.
    • The front mapped house outline is about 9.1 m from the street-side parcel boundary. This is not a surveyed curb-to-wall measurement.

    What you can change

    Independent hedge heights, planting beds, garden fill, floor and eye heights, observer position, terrace depth and canopy geometry. Optional building height and distance adjustments are comparison scenarios; resetting them restores the mapped data exactly. Budget inputs and building changes are included in shared links and scenario comparisons. The house floor stays fixed when you raise the garden. New hedges move with the fill only when the corresponding switch is on.

    How screening is calculated

    Rays run from the selected eye position to sampled facade and roof points. Finite hedge screens, the terrace canopy and approximate surrounding building volumes can intercept them. The percentage is the share of those sampled points blocked; it is not the percentage of your visual field or a guarantee for every point in the garden.

    The house/window openings and ornamental planting are not reliable surveyed obstructions, so they do not contribute to the coverage score. The thin high returns near PCK 3 are excluded by default; enable them in the assumptions for a sensitivity check. Nearby roof points include sampled rooftop details; the two farther blocks use only median roof levels. The score covers all five target buildings from the observer, regardless of the camera direction.

    Geometry & proposals

    The 8.5 m terrace, pool, jacuzzi, glazing, architectural finishes and furniture illustrate the design. House and nearby roof meshes follow the available mapped roof samples; their triangulated surfaces remain approximations. Foliage, glazing, materials and furniture are illustrative design details, not measured assets. Canopy rays use a finite roof and front/side fascia. The budget derives rear-garden fill quantities after excluding the pool, terrace and any retained garage. Soil, placing, delivery, access, drainage and retaining allowances are editable. Quantities are planning estimates; retaining and drainage allowances are not an engineered design. Thuja pricing uses published size bands up to the verified limit, with editable supplier quotes for larger stock.

    Sun and shade

    The date and time controls use Europe/Warsaw, including daylight saving, regardless of your browser location. The site location and true-north correction come from its EPSG:2180 coordinates. The moving sun uses the NOAA solar-position equations. Rendered shadows follow the detailed 3D meshes; surface-point checks use conservative mapped roof envelopes and ideal solid hedges. Possible roof shade identifies cases where only the upper envelope blocks the ray. Clouds, atmospheric light scattering, neighbouring trees, porous foliage and isolated unverified high roof returns are not simulated. The high-return switch remains a privacy-only sensitivity check. A sunlight check at one point does not describe an entire area. Shared links and exported scenarios preserve the selected solar date and clock.
