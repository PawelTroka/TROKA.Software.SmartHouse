Planning position & sources

These are design concepts for review. Gdańsk’s landscape resolution sets a 6 m ceiling for genuine small garden architecture (§5(2)); classification depends on the actual size, construction and function. It does not approve a 6 m privacy wall.

Fences in single-family areas are generally limited to 2.2 m and at least 35% openness, subject to the resolution’s exceptions. Calling a fence a pergola or raising its base does not establish permission. Canopies and raised terraces need their own planning and construction checks.

Confirm the local plan, foundations, boundaries and rights to the municipal rear strip before building. Dense planting is an established-coverage scenario, not a promise that new climbers will screen within a year.

Levels, vehicle assumptions & sources

The living floor is an editable assumption above the mapped garden reference. The basement floor follows that level minus the slab allowance, clear height and any extra lowering. Street level comes from the terrain grid; a kerb, basement and foundation survey is still needed.

MG’s published laden ground clearance is extended across the full car length as a conservative underside envelope. Actual bumpers, tyres, loading, scissor-door clearance and roof operation need vehicle measurements. The model does not assess wet or icy ramp grip; MG specifies AWD for forward travel only.

Former Polish Technical Conditions provide comparison benchmarks: 25% for an individual-garage ramp, 3 m access width, 2.2 m garage clear height and a 2.3 × 2.0 m opening. These are not a low-car clearance approval. The former regulation was repealed in September 2026; applicability through the statutory transitional route must be confirmed for the project.

The local plan, road connection, boundaries, foundations, groundwater, drainage and any threshold remain project-design questions. A positive car-clearance reserve does not verify these items or the local headroom.
