# Garden design styles for the three Gdańsk properties

## What each concept represents

Each concept is an original proposal for the house, garden and the way the rooms are furnished. The catalogue translates architectural precedents into material choices, furniture construction, seating groups, dining arrangements and evening light. Timber decking, clinker paving and large stone slabs have different joint rhythms; sofas, daybeds and chairs also occupy different compositions.

The complete-renovation setting coordinates the interior furniture, architectural finishes and owner's exterior. **Current design** restores the original house arrangement. Styles furnish the selected renovation plan rather than moving its walls or wet fittings. The house footprint, selected terrace and spa geometry, two-car garage study, screening trees, measured neighbourhood and analytical sightline model remain controlled by their existing data and settings.

The resort arrangements reserve a clear terrace threshold and a route to the garden steps, then fit a conversation group, reclining pair or daybed into the available quiet bay. Poolside furniture stays within its existing furnishing zone. Dining orientation, small planter groups and lamp emphasis change with the style. Compact decks receive a reduced furniture group. These are planning layouts, not an accessibility or construction certification.

Procurement, labour, finishes and bespoke fabrication require product-specific quotations. The budget keeps the base finish allowances and provides a separate incremental style quote. Its default of zero means the upgrade is unquoted.

## Living as a private retreat

The shared design direction is a usable sequence of spaces: an open route from the house, a sheltered place to sit, a dining group, poolside rest and the existing sauna and plunge. The furniture groups vary by style. Facing platforms suit Japandi; a daybed and loose chair suit Bali and Beach House; curved conversation groups suit Organic Modern; a larger social salon suits Party Club and Riviera Deco. Bedroom reading chairs, low service furniture and shaded lamps make the interiors feel occupied without filling the circulation routes.

Two contemporary hospitality projects informed that approach. Norm Architects describes filtered separation, natural materials and deliberately subdued artificial light at [Sjöparken](https://normcph.com/project/sjoparken/). Amankila's [Garden Pool Pavilion](https://www.aman.com/resorts/amankila/accommodation/pavilion/garden-pool-pavilion) combines a private pool courtyard, terrace, daybed and adjoining living space. Garden Lab uses these as spatial and lighting references. It does not copy their layouts or import their photographed assets.

Evening compositions give the dining table and resting places more emphasis than the perimeter. Renovation styles use fewer hedge accents, selected tree uplights, low step lighting and dimmed surface illumination. Their fixture forms and relative intensities vary; the existing light controls continue to dim the installed proposal. The original design retains its earlier arrangement.

## Gdańsk exposure and procurement

- **Winter exposure.** IMGW’s 1991–2020 normal for the Gdańsk station gives a mean daily minimum of −3.3 °C in January and −2.7 °C in February. These are station statistics. A pool surround or sheltered courtyard may have a different surface temperature and freeze pattern. [IMGW minimum temperature](https://klimat.imgw.pl/pl/climate-normals/TMIN_AVE)
- **Walking and splash surfaces.** Specify the exact exterior product, declared frost behaviour, and suitable wet or barefoot slip performance for the location. Include joints, falls, drainage and edge detailing in the installed quote. As an example of the available evidence, Paradyż publishes frost resistance, the R10 slip class and outdoor joint instructions for a named clinker product. That data describes the listed variant. Poolside barefoot use needs its own product assessment. [Ceramika Paradyż](https://www.paradyz.com/en/products/p65716-sundown-terra-mat-elewacja-245x66-g1)
- **Boards and soffits.** Allow for drainage below timber decking, ventilation, swelling gaps, appropriate fixings and access for cleaning. Board spacing and joint width in the visual are illustrative. Use the chosen system’s installation guide for the eventual assembly. [Thermory installation guide](https://thermory.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Final-with-onepager_25.03.24_Thermory_INSTALLATION_GUIDE_decking-boards_0324_ENG.pdf)
- **Exposed metal.** Salt deposition, sheltered surfaces and the frequency of rain washing influence grade and maintenance requirements. A coating or metallic colour in the viewer gives no corrosion-resistance rating. Ask for the exposure and maintenance assumptions for each proposed finish, especially sheltered fascia, fasteners and areas beside chlorinated water. [Outokumpu](https://www.outokumpu.com/en/expertise/2025/stainless-steel-and-atmospheric-corrosion-what-you-need-to-know)
- **Plants.** Coastal wind and salt exposure vary with shelter, and plant hardiness depends on local winter conditions. The Bali, Mediterranean and desert-inspired concepts derive their character from materials, light and shade geometry. The selected Polish garden programme and planting selections carry through unchanged. [RHS coastal planting](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/for-places/coastal-areas) · [IMGW climate normals](https://klimat.imgw.pl/pl/climate-normals/)
- **Outdoor lighting.** DarkSky and the Illuminating Engineering Society recommend targeted, low-level, controlled and warm-coloured lighting. The resort recipes use that direction to reduce perimeter emphasis and focus light on occupied areas, while retaining the independent dimmers. A warm lens or shield shape illustrates an intention. The rendered light sources are not product photometry; selected fixtures, measured aiming, spill and illuminance would be required to assess installed performance. [DarkSky / IES](https://darksky.org/resources/guides-and-how-tos/lighting-principles/)

## Comparing concepts in the simulator

Hold the property, programme, view, chosen light circuits, date and time constant. From an elevated garden view compare the seating groups, dining direction, clear routes and poolside resting places. At eye level compare joinery, upholstery, canopy details and the relationship between the interior lounge and terrace. At blue hour compare the table, steps, focal trees and darker spaces between them. The complete-renovation switch coordinates the house and furniture; dimensions, observers, screening and mapped context remain available through their own controls.
