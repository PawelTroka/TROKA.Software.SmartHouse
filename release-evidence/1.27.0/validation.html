What the model establishes

Visible proposals, explicit limits.

Archived source, current survey needed

Printed room areas, reconstructed polygons and mapped registration are kept distinct. Present-day openings, wall construction, stair geometry and exterior alignment still need an as-built check.

Structural work remains a project

Broad garden glazing, removed partitions, the large balcony and spa loads need structural design, especially around the documented timber floor. The viewer does not certify load capacity or demolition feasibility.

The cellar is still a constraint

The original section shows about 1.70 m clear height. The explicit two-car study displays the lowered floor and ramp along with retained stair/flue conflicts. Excavation, underpinning and drainage remain unresolved design work.
